Use case name: ShowFollowedAuthorsFeed
Description: The personal feed shows only articles written by the authors the reader follows, newest first, and every article list reports the real total number of matching articles so its page numbers are correct.
Participating Actors:
- Reader: a signed-in user.
- Visitor: a person who is not signed in.
Entry conditions: The Reader is signed in and follows at least one author who has published articles.
Flow of events User System:
1. The Reader opens the home page.
2. The system shows the personal feed: only articles written by authors the Reader follows, most recent first, ten articles per page, with page numbers computed from the total number of such articles.
3. The Reader chooses another page number.
4. The system shows the articles of that page, still most recent first, and marks the chosen page as current.
5. The Reader opens the profile of an author they follow and stops following them, then returns to the home page.
6. The system shows the personal feed without the articles of that author, and the page numbers reflect the new total.
Alternative flows:
A1. The Reader follows nobody, or the authors they follow have no articles. The system shows an empty personal feed with a message that there are no articles yet and points the Reader to the global feed.
A2. The Reader's own articles are not part of their personal feed.
A3. The Reader opens the global feed, a tag filter, an author's articles or an author's favorited articles. The system shows those articles most recent first, with page numbers computed from the total number of matching articles.
A4. A Visitor requests the personal feed directly from the API. The system refuses the request as unauthenticated (HTTP 401).
Exit conditions:
- The personal feed contains exactly the articles of followed authors, newest first.
- For every article list, the reported total does not depend on which page was requested, and every page number shown leads to a non-empty page.
Quality requirements:
- Changing pages keeps the reader's place in the list visible: the page selector shows which page is current.
